BayLeaf Sandboxes

A small computer in the cloud for trying things out

Your sandbox is a separate computer that runs only for you. Use it to try unfamiliar software, especially AI tools that work with your files, without installing anything on your own computer. You use its apps in your browser.

Kept separate

Software in your sandbox can’t see your personal computer. It only works with files you upload or create there.

Shared files

Every app below works in the same workspace folder. Upload a file once, then use it in any of them.

Sleeps when you’re done

After you stop using it, your sandbox goes to sleep and keeps your saved files. When you return, waking it can take a few extra seconds.

Your sandbox is awake. It will go to sleep about an hour after you close its apps.

Apps you can open

Open as many or as few as you like. Each opens in a new tab.

These are independent open-source projects that BayLeaf sets up for you. If you like one, you can often install it on your own computer later.

AI agents

An AI agent can do more than answer questions in chat. It can read and write files, run software, and carry out multi-step tasks in your sandbox.

  • OpenChamber

    https://openchamber.dev/

    Build and work on projects with an agent that can plan, edit many files, and show you its changes as it goes.

    OpenRunning
    Using OpenChamber here

    Your agent uses AI models through BayLeaf and shares your BayLeaf usage allowance. Its conversations are saved in your sandbox, separately from BayLeaf Chat.

    It runs in your sandbox only while you’re connected, so it can’t run scheduled tasks or reply to messaging apps while your sandbox is asleep.

    Not sure how to do something? Ask the agent. It knows about your sandbox and can help you set up other software.

  • Nanobot

    https://github.com/HKUDS/nanobot

    Talk with a lighter-weight agent in a simple chat window. It’s good for quick questions about your files and short tasks.

Not limited to this list

Your sandbox is a whole computer, so you can install other software too. The apps above are convenient starting points we’ve heard people ask for. Ask an agent in OpenChamber or Nanobot to help you set up something else.

If an app becomes part of your routine, consider installing it on your own computer. Each project’s site explains how.

Files, storage & privacy

Sandboxes unused for 90 days are deleted, along with their files and app histories. Download anything you want to keep.

What happens when my sandbox sleeps?

Saved files, settings, and app histories are kept. Running programs stop, including any task an agent was in the middle of. Unsaved work in an open app may be lost.

Opening any app wakes your sandbox. Signing in here also gives it a nudge, so it’s often ready by the time you choose an app.

Who can see what’s in my sandbox?

Your apps are private links that only work while you’re signed in. AI requests go to providers that don’t keep your prompts or responses.

Files and app histories in your sandbox are stored, and BayLeaf’s operators can technically access that storage. Read the privacy notice for details.

Can I use my sandbox from BayLeaf Chat?

Yes. BayLeaf Chat can read and write the same sandbox files. Its conversations are separate from those in OpenChamber and Nanobot.
