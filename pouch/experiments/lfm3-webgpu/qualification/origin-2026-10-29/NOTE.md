# Original LFM3 qualification (2026-10-29)

The first browser WebGPU qualification of LFM3, run in the main checkout after the
original worktree was lost, before this branch's restore. Its REPORT.md carries the
offline-reload, offline-restart and CSP (`connect-src` API-only, no wasm-eval)
evidence that later Pouch work relied on. Kept verbatim on 2026-11-01 when the
untracked copy was removed; its browser profiles, weights (identical to the pinned,
hash-verified LFM3 files) and node_modules were not kept. Paths inside refer to the
original layout.
