What can BayLeaf see?

Your conversations are stored only in this browser. Each request is encrypted here, to an enclave whose hardware attestation and published code this page just verified. BayLeaf relays ciphertext and sees who you are (your key, or your IP under Campus Pass), when you send, how many bytes, and which model ran. It cannot read prompts or replies. The weakest link is this page itself: compare its SHA-256 with the published release.
